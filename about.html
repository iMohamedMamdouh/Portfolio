About me

Mobile apps, AI automation, and growth that actually moves sales.

I'm a Cairo-based developer working across three closely connected worlds: Flutter mobile apps, AI automation on n8n, and growth hacking. I currently lead AI Automation & Growth at Wasilaah, after spending six months as a Fulfillment Specialist there — which gave me a hands-on view of how Odoo and Shopify really run on a busy floor.

That mix means I don't stop at building a screen or a workflow — I follow it through to the metric it moves. In my current role I lifted sales by 20% in two months by reshaping the website experience and running SMS + email campaigns, and I built an ROI tracking system on top of our customer database so every campaign reports its own return.

On the engineering side, I work in Clean Architecture for mobile and treat automations the same way: small, observable pieces wired together. I care a lot about how things behave on slow networks, busy operations and devices that get dropped.
